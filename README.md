# PACIFIC RIM (2013)

## General Information

**Pacific Rim** is a science-fiction action movie directed by **Guillermo del Toro** and released in 2013.

*The movie focuses on humanity's fight against giant monsters called Kaiju.*

> "When the world is under attack, humanity must stand together to survive."

---

## Movie Details

- **Title:** Pacific Rim
- **Release Date:** July 12, 2013
- **Director:** Guillermo del Toro
- **Genre:** Science Fiction, Action, Adventure
- **Country:** United States

## Main Characters

1. Raleigh Becket
2. Mako Mori
3. Stacker Pentecost
4. Newton Geiszler
5. Hermann Gottlieb

## Important Terms

- **Kaiju** - Giant monsters that attack humanity.
- **Jaegers** - Giant robots created to fight the Kaiju.
- **Drifting** - A process that connects the minds of two pilots.
- **Shatterdome** - A base where Jaegers are maintained.

## Lessons from the Movie

1. Teamwork
2. Courage
3. Trust
4. Unity
5. Sacrifice

## Important Message

The movie teaches us that people can overcome difficult challenges through teamwork, courage, and unity.

**Conclusion:** Pacific Rim shows that humanity becomes stronger when people work together toward one common goal.
